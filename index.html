Where the system is straining — and where it has room

Capacity is real surgical throughput (volume per 100k of the province's 65+ population); demand is a real, independent pressure signal — the year-over-year growth rate of that same 65+ population. The two are different units on purpose: a province can have fine current per-capita throughput while its demand curve is about to outrun it. The dotted crosshairs mark the median capacity and median demand across this province set. Color is the wait residual (actual vs. expected wait, from a regression on capacity and demand) — a province's position tells you its circumstances, its color tells you whether it's beating or missing what those circumstances predict.
