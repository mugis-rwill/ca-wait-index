Zone boundaries are peer-relative (split at this hospital set's own median), independent of the capacity=demand diagonal, which is an absolute physical reference. The two can disagree — a hospital can be "above median capacity" for this set while still running a utilization ratio above 1. That gap is deliberate: median position says how you compare to peers, the diagonal says whether you're structurally keeping up with your own demand.
