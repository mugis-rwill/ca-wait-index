Chart 1 · headline

Ninetieth-percentile wait in days, 2025, ordered. Hip and knee are drawn separately because their orderings are not the same — Saskatchewan is third-slowest on hip and fifth on knee. Matched pairs

Chart 3 · every surviving pair

Province pairs whose volumes fall within 5% of each other, in the same procedure and year, on the same reporting window and provenance type. The rule was fixed before the results were seen. Every pair it produced is drawn here, including the ones with small gaps — none were dropped for being unremarkable, and the charts are ordered alphabetically rather than by gap size.

Read the line, not the dots. Each province gets a dot at its median and a dot at its 90th percentile, joined by a line. The line's length is the tail — the distance between the typical patient and the unlucky one. Two provinces can share a median and still hand a payer very different bills.
All 18 surviving pair-years, as a table

No chart on this page shows a residual, a fitted value, or a quadrant. Every mark is a number CIHI published, drawn to scale.

No confidence intervals are shown. CIHI receives aggregate percentiles submitted by provincial ministries, not case-level records, and publishes no intervals of its own — so there is no sampling distribution to draw one from. None were manufactured. Figures are April–September counts and are not annualized.
