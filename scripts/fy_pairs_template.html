Different Waits

The matching rule holds case volume still between two provinces. Everything that moves after that is the finding.

What changed

window swap

Same rule, same code, different input table: CIHI's 12-month fiscal-year rows (2020FY–2024FY) instead of the 6-month April–September rows. Nothing about the criterion was adjusted.

Far fewer pairs survive

6 of 450 pair-years, against 18 of 540 on April–September. A full year of volume is harder for two provinces to match than half a year — more time means more room to diverge.

The gaps are smaller too

Median absolute P90 gap 76 days, against 144 on April–September. The range runs 9 to 204 days. Two of the six pairs are close to identical on waits — exactly the unremarkable pairs the brief asked to see.

The Nova Scotia / Saskatchewan pair is gone

Not because it failed the rule, but because 2025FY does not exist — the fiscal year had not closed at publication. That pair matched in 2025 only, so the fiscal-year window cannot contain it. Its absence here is a calendar fact, not evidence against it.

One province-year is missing entirely

Newfoundland and Labrador published no 2024FY figures for either procedure — all four metrics are n/a (Table 1 rows 18790–18797). That province-year is absent rather than imputed, so the fiscal-year table holds 98 rows, not 100, and Newfoundland cannot be paired in 2024.
